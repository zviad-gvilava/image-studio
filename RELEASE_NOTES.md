## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-23

### Create images and know what they cost

- Describe an image, choose its size and quality, and optionally add reference images.
- See the price range before you generate, and exactly how it's worked out.
- Pay the image's exact cost once it's ready. Failed generations are always free.

### Follow every image as it happens

- Your history updates by itself while images are being made: submitting, queued, processing, done or failed.
- Each image shows its estimate next to what you were actually charged.
- If something goes wrong, you see why in plain words, and can try again in one click.

### Credits

- Keep a credit balance in US dollars and top it up from the Credits page (purchases are simulated in this demo).
- Money for images still in progress is set aside, so you can never spend more than you have.

### For operators

- A profit report shows what users were charged, what Pixelforge charged, and the profit for each day and user, and confirms each day against Pixelforge's own records.
